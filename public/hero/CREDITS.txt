Third-party assets used by the site
===================================

aircraft.glb
  Airbus A330-300 model from Flightradar24's open 3D model set
  https://github.com/Flightradar24/fr24-3d-models  (models/a333.glb)
  Derived from the FlightGear / FGMEMBERS A330-300 aircraft.
  License: GNU GPL v2. Converted from glTF 1.0 to glTF 2.0; geometry and textures unchanged.
  If this model is redistributed, keep this notice and the GPL v2 terms with it.

../about/aircraft-dusk.webp
  Side-on render of the same A330-300 model, used on the About page. Same source and licence as above.

sky.jpg, env.hdr, ../sim/sky-360.jpg  (sky.jpg is also the About page's sky; sky-360.jpg is the simulator's)
  Cropped, colour-graded and tonemapped from the HDRI "The Sky Is On Fire" on Poly Haven
  https://polyhaven.com/a/the_sky_is_on_fire
  License: CC0 1.0 (public domain).
  sky-360.jpg is the full circle of the same photograph with the same grade; the promenade it was
  taken from (buildings, a hill, lamp posts) has been painted out so only sky remains.

Fonts (bundled via @fontsource)
  Cormorant Garamond and Inter, both SIL Open Font License 1.1.

../horizon/cargo.glb  (the transport flown in the simulator, and the one that delivers the car)
  Lockheed C-130 Hercules model by Emmanuel Baranger (2008) from the FlightGear aircraft collection
  https://github.com/FGMEMBERS/C130  (Models/c130.ac, Interior/interior.ac, Propellers/propeller.ac)
  License: GNU GPL v2. Converted from AC3D to glTF 2.0 with its default livery; the landing gear and
  its doors are left out, and the propellers are redrawn as discs.
  If this model is redistributed, keep this notice and the GPL v2 terms with it.

../horizon/coupe.glb, ../horizon/wheel.webp  (the car)
  "Kanagawa Z35" sports car from Speed Dreams (sc-kanagawa-z35gts)
  https://forge.a-lec.org/speed-dreams/sc-kanagawa-z35gts
  Made by William Brun (CC BY); rebuilt in low poly and adapted for Speed Dreams by W-E-C;
  texture and mesh improvements copyright 2012 Eckhard M. Jaeger.
  License: Free Art License (https://artlibre.org). Converted from AC3D to glTF 2.0; repainted blue
  in the page, wheels rebuilt from the model's wheel texture.
  If this model is redistributed, keep this notice and the Free Art License terms with it.
